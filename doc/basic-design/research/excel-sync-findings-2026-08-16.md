- → **実質「転換前のウォーム待機へ回帰」**。大阪の常時コストが発生する

出典: [AWS Backup PITR](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-backup/latest/devguide/point-in-time-recovery.html) / [Aurora PITR](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonRDS/latest/AuroraUserGuide/aurora-pitr.html)
